# Authors

## Original Tribes2Wiki contributors

The articles in this repository were written by the community of the original
**tribes2wiki.com** between 2008 and 2011. Where the Internet Archive preserved a page's
revision history, those edits are attributed to their authors in this repository's git
history, with the original edit date as the commit's author date.

This list is necessarily incomplete. Only 8 of the wiki's 121 pages have a surviving
`action=history` capture, so the great majority of contributions cannot be attributed to
anyone by name. If you contributed to the original wiki and are missing or misattributed
here, please open an issue.

### About the commit email addresses

The original wiki never published contributor email addresses. MediaWiki keeps them
private, and no `User:` page, `Special:ListUsers` or `Special:Contributions` capture
survives, so there is nothing to recover.

Commits therefore use proxy addresses of the form `github+<username>@tribes2.dev`, which
are controlled by the Tribes 2 Community organisation. **These are not the contributors'
real email addresses** — they exist only to give git a stable identity to attribute
commits to.

### About anonymous attribution

Two different situations both resolve to a single `Anonymous Tribes2Wiki contributor`
identity in the git history:

**Logged-out edits.** The original wiki recorded these against the editor's IP address. IP
addresses are personal data, and writing them into a permanent public git history goes
further than the original wiki ever did, so they are collapsed rather than published.

**Recovered pages with no surviving revision history.** For all but eight of the wiki's
pages the archive preserved the article but not the record of who wrote it. That writing
is the original community's work, so it is attributed anonymously — **not** to whoever ran
the restoration. The person performing the import is the committer on those commits, never
the author.
