# Asset credits

## `emblem.svg`, `public/favicon.svg`, `brand/org-avatar.png`

The Tribes 2 twin-fork emblem, vectorised by `npm run trace-emblem` from the community
avatar in [ChocoTaco1/PlayT2-Modern](https://github.com/ChocoTaco1/PlayT2-Modern)
(`images/avatar.svg`), in which the emblem appears as a cutout through a solid disc.

The source art is **not vendored** into this repository — the trace script fetches it on
demand into the gitignored `reference/` folder. `PlayT2-Modern` declares no licence, and
its authors keep their copyright in the original artwork.

The emblem itself derives from *Tribes 2*, a trademark of its respective rights holders.
Used here for a non-commercial community project. See [`NOTICE.md`](../../NOTICE.md).
