The **Juggernaut armor**, more commonly referred to as **heavy armor** or **heavy**, is the largest, slowest, and most durable armor in Tribes 2. The lack of maneuverability limits the use of heavy armor to tasks where speed is not required - though there are exceptions. Heavy is most useful in situations where you are likely to take a lot of punishment such as defending your base or taking over the enemy base. Heavy armor is also the only armor that can carry a [mortar](/weapons/fusion-mortar "Fusion mortar"), which is a key weapon when it comes to attacking the enemy base.
