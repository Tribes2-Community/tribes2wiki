**Blair Lag Project** is a [Classic](/mods/classic) server run by **Blakhart**, populated
with a large number of bots.

The bots are the point. There are a lot of them, and they are capable of genuinely
impressive combat behaviour rather than the wandering targets bots are often assumed to be.
That makes for a full, busy server without a lobby of other players in it.

## Why play there

Not every session needs to be competitive. Blair Lag Project is aimed at giving players a
calm and quiet place to play when PvP isn't the mood you are in — somewhere to fly around,
practise, or just have a game without the pressure of a
[PUG](/community) or a full pub.

It is also useful if you want to work on something specific. Bots will keep a match running
while you practise routes, [skiing](/tactics/skiing) or shots, without needing enough people
online to fill a server.

## Playing there

The server appears on the **JOIN** tab once you have the game and a
[community patch](/patches) installed — see [Tribes 2](/start/tribes-2) for setup.

Being a Classic server, it plays to the Classic ruleset rather than
[Base](/mods/base). The modding handbook covers the difference in
[Classic](https://modding.tribes2wiki.com/37-classic/) and
[AI bots](https://modding.tribes2wiki.com/05-gameplay-systems/ai-bots.html).
