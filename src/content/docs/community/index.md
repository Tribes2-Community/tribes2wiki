## Pubs and PUGs

Two kinds of game, and the difference matters if you're new:

A **pub** is a public server. Join whenever, play whoever turns up, leave when you like.
Legacy CTF+ and Dad Tribes are the ones to try first.

A **PUG** — a pick-up game — runs to a **schedule**, but the teams are not settled until
the match itself. Attendance varies, so sides are picked from whoever actually turns up,
right at the start. PUGs are where the more competitive play happens, and the schedule is
announced in Discord rather than by sitting in a server waiting. The Cut runs Saturday
nights, Dad Tribes runs Fridays, and Ski Club runs 5v5 through
[The Cut's Discord](https://discord.gg/yz3AhkJCxg).

**Tournaments** are the exception: there the teams are drafted well in advance, and the
fixtures are known before the season starts. See the
[25 Year Anniversary Tournament](https://tribes2forever.com).

If a server looks empty, that's usually why — check Discord before assuming the game is
dead.
