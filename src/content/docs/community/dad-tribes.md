**Dad Tribes** does two jobs. It is a general **public server** you can join whenever, and
it hosts a **Friday PUG** — organised pick-up games to get a proper match going as the
weekend starts.

It is run by **Jerry** and **Gunther**.

The Friday slot is a good one to know about. Pick-up games get teams picked and everyone in
at a set time, which beats waiting in a server hoping enough people turn up. Between Dad
Tribes on Fridays and [| The Cut |](/community/the-cut) on Saturdays, most weekends have
organised play at both ends.

## Playing there

The public server is on the **JOIN** tab once you have the game and a
[community patch](/patches/) installed — see [Tribes 2](/start/tribes-2). The Friday PUG is
organised through Discord, linked from [PlayT2.com](https://playt2.com).
