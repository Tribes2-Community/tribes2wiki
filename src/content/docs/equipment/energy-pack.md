![](/images/Energy_pack.png)

Screenshot of an energy pack.

The energy pack is a pack that has the passive effect of increasing the speed with which your energy recharges, thereby increasing jet mobility and improving the use of weapons that use your energy. It is also required in order to use a [laser rifle](/weapons/laser-rifle "Laser rifle"). Due to the importance of managing energy, the energy pack is considered the best pack in the game and is almost always used as standard equipment.
