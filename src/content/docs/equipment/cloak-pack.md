Screenshot of a cloak pack.

The **cloak pack**, when active, renders the wearer invisible to other players and any sensor other than motion sensors and cameras (see below). When inactive, the cloak pack will still prevent the player from being detected by pulse sensors; however, turrets can still fire upon them if an enemy player is looking at them.

Due to a bug in Tribes 2, mods that give cameras the ability to detect players using a sensor jammer pack will also give cameras the ability to detect cloaked players, even if the cloak is active.
