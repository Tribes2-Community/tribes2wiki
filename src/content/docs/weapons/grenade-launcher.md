Official [browser](/reference/browser "Browser") art of the grenade launcher.

The **grenade launcher**, abbreviated **GL**, is just what it sounds like. It fires grenades in arc that will explode on impact after a certain amount of "fuse time" has passed. It fires relatively quickly - about the same rate as the plasma rifle. The speed of the projectile and size of the arc depends on what [mod](/mods/mod "Mod") you are playing. The fast firing rate and highly damaging explosive projectiles make the grenade launcher one of the most effective weapons in the game at killing [cappers](/tactics/capper "Capper") or attacking someone at medium range.
