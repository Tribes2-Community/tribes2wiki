Tribes 2 shipped in 2001 and its official servers are long gone, but the game is still
played and the community that kept it alive is still around. These are the places worth
knowing about — both for playing and for research when writing an article.

:::caution[Before you copy anything]
Everything linked here belongs to whoever wrote it. Forum posts and guides are their
authors' work, so please don't paste text from these sites into an article without asking.
Read them, then write what you know in your own words — facts about how the game plays
aren't anyone's property, only the particular words used to describe them.

Where another author's material does appear on a page, it is credited there, and they keep
their copyright in it. If an author would rather their work were not here, say so and it
will be removed.
:::

## TribesNEXT

<https://www.tribesnext.com>

The community project that brought Tribes 2 back after the official authentication servers
shut down, replacing them so the game could keep running. Its forums are the most active
Tribes 2 community still going, covering patches, server hosting, troubleshooting and
general play.

Of particular interest to this wiki is
[**A newbie's guide**](https://www.tribesnext.com/forum/discussion/340/a-newbie-039-s-guide),
posted by **WiiMote** in January 2009 — a long introduction to the game covering armours,
vehicles, weapons, packs, base assets, skiing and the game's terminology, with a lengthy
discussion thread underneath. It covers the ground of roughly a third of the articles
missing from this wiki, which makes it a good place to jog your memory before writing one.

The thread also links to the original tribes2wiki.com — **Kryand**, who was the original
wiki's most prolific contributor, turns up there pointing newcomers toward it.

## The Construct

<https://www.the-construct.net>

Home of the Tribes 2 **Construction Mod**, a long-running community mod built around
in-game building rather than combat, with its own
[forums](https://www.the-construct.net/forums/) and a collection of players' creations.

The original wiki linked a `Construction` article that was never written. Anyone who knows
this mod is well placed to finally write it.

## PlayT2

<https://github.com/ChocoTaco1/PlayT2-Modern>

A modern guide site for getting into Tribes 2 — installation, server lists, map packs,
textures and illustrated guides to weapons, packs, deployables and vehicles.

Its guide artwork replaced 13 of the images lost when the original wiki went down; its
authors keep their copyright in it. See
[the image credits](https://github.com/Tribes2-Community/tribes2wiki/blob/main/src/assets/images/CREDITS.md)
for the full mapping.

## The Internet Archive

<https://web.archive.org>

The reason this wiki exists at all. Every article recovered here came from its captures of
tribes2wiki.com, and each imported page links back to the exact capture it was recovered
from, at the bottom of the page. See [The restoration](/project/restoration) for the
detail.
