The **tribe and warrior browser**, referred to as simply the **browser** or occasionally **TWB**, was the in-game system that kept track of basic player and team information. When a player first registered his account name, a browser page was created and tied to it. A player's browser page contained their registration date, a website link of their choice, a small picture in the upper left chosen from a predefined list, a buddy list, a list of tribes, a large portion of the page that players could design to describe themselves, and a history of all browser-related events in which that player has been involved. Additionally, a player could use the browser to create or join a tribe. A tribe's browser page was mostly the same as a player's browser page as far as content goes. From the browser, a player could change his tribe tag to that of any of his tribes, as well as manage the members of each of his tribes if he was a high enough rank in that tribe. Finally, in the first couple years of the game, every tribe was given a private forum in which the members could discuss whatever they wanted.
