# Notice and provenance

## Where this content came from

The articles in this repository were recovered from **tribes2wiki.com**, a MediaWiki 1.16
community wiki for the 2001 game *Tribes 2*. The site went offline and its domain
subsequently lapsed; the content here was recovered from captures held by the
[Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/).

Every imported page records its own provenance in frontmatter:

```yaml
source:
  url: http://www.tribes2wiki.com/wiki/Spinfusor
  archived: https://web.archive.org/web/20111206131713id_/...
  lastModified: '2011-05-26T10:11:00.000Z'
```

That provenance is also shown in the footer of each imported page on the site, linking
back to the archived original.

## Licensing

The original wiki **declared no content licence** — its footer carried no copyright or
licence statement, which is what MediaWiki does when `$wgRightsText` is left unset. Those
authors keep their copyright in what they wrote.

There is deliberately **no single site-wide content licence**. Material here arrives on
different terms, so each page carries its own — see
[CONTENT-LICENSING.md](CONTENT-LICENSING.md). Site code and import scripts are
**[MIT](LICENSE)**.

This matters most for material from other authors: forum guides and the like remain the
copyright of whoever wrote them, and pages carrying such material credit it.

Contributors to the original wiki are credited in [`AUTHORS.md`](AUTHORS.md) and in this
repository's git history. If you are one of the original authors and object to your work
being republished here, please open an issue and it will be removed.
